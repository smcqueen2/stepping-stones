Sources and what the readings mean

River and tide: Natural Resources Wales, unverified telemetry. The Bridgend gauge is upstream; Newport is a regional tide reference. Their metres use different reference levels and do not give water depth at the stones. Gaps in the record are left visible.

Precipitation: Open-Meteo archived weather-model output, not a local rain-gauge measurement. Recent values can be revised.
