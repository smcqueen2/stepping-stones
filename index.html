History of the stones and area

The stepping stones beside Ogmore Castle have long been used as a practical river crossing, linking routes between Ogmore-by-Sea, Merthyr Mawr, and surrounding paths. Their uneven, historic structure means crossing difficulty can vary stone-by-stone.

Ogmore Castle itself dates to the Norman period and sits beside the river where tide influence and upstream rainfall can combine to alter river conditions quickly.

Note: historic details should be reviewed against local records and heritage sources before publication updates.

About this project

Built by a local resident to help walkers, runners, and cyclists avoid surprises at the crossing. This is a free, open-source community tool.

The model is deliberately cautious and uses “likely” language to avoid false certainty.
