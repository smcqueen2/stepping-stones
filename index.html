About this project

A crossing shaped by the river.

Built by a local resident to help walkers, runners, and cyclists avoid surprises at the crossing. This is a free, open-source community tool.

The model is deliberately cautious and uses “likely” language to avoid false certainty.

The painting illustrates a combined river, tide and rainfall prediction. It does not measure the depth at each stone or guarantee safe passage.

Local readings are metres at each station, with no datum supplied by this feed. They are separate measurements, not depth over the stones. Penybont measures a tidal river, not coastal sea level. The crossing model still uses Bridgend and the regional coastal gauge; its calibration has not been transferred to the new stations.

Official NRW warnings & monitored areas

We check two local warning areas and two broader alert areas. A wider-area alert does not establish flooding at the stones. Current warnings remain relevant when you view a future model estimate. The absence of a warning does not mean the crossing is safe.

  • Tidal area at Ogmore Castle
  • River Ewenny at Ewenny Village · includes Ogmore Castle
  • Rivers Llynfi and Ogmore · wider catchment
  • Swansea Bay and the Gower coast · wider coast

High and low compare with the previous 48 hours of gauge readings, rather than the depth at the stones.

Home-page tide times are approximate coastal high/low estimates derived from hourly sea-level forecasts, rounded to half an hour. They describe the Ogmore coast, not when the stones will submerge or become passable. Coastal model accuracy is limited, particularly inside estuaries. All tide times use local Welsh time (GMT/BST).

Tide timing data: Open-Meteo Marine, generated using E.U. Copernicus Marine Service Information. Timing estimates are our adaptation of data supplied under CC BY 4.0. The regional NRW gauge still supplies the crossing model's tide input.
