About this project

A crossing shaped by the river.

Built by a local resident to help walkers, runners, and cyclists avoid surprises at the crossing. This is a free, open-source community tool.

The model is deliberately cautious and uses “likely” language to avoid false certainty.

The painting illustrates a combined river, tide and rainfall prediction. It does not measure the depth at each stone or guarantee safe passage.
